# Treatment: "What can developers build on Seismic?"

**Structure:** three candidates: (1) a code editor where each use case is typed; (2) a list that turns, laid out as a table of five models; (3) a city of apps seen from above. **Chosen: (2).** The post is a list, so the film makes the list physical: five turntables on one table, visited in a single continuous move.

**Logline:** on a white table of five working models, each app shows its numbers through clear glass until the glass frosts over. In the end the whole frame frosts: the next generation needs confidentiality.

**Native move:** frost. A glass panel stays physically in place while what's behind it stops being readable, which is exactly what confidentiality means. It plays once per station (a wipe), once in reverse (everything clears: "transparency"), and once over the whole frame (the frost wave at the peak).

**Different from the first film (`../seismic/`):** light vs dark stage · one camera track vs cuts · flat logo stamp vs a 3D stone · mallet process music vs trailer braams · an ending that fades to white vs a fade to black · mask-rise and wipe type vs blur-slide type.

Full-frame moves: 1.0 (title slam), 10.5 (table reveal), 52.0 (frost wave). Silence: 51.55–52.0.
Score: F-major colour (Fmaj7 · Dm7 · Bbmaj7 · G). Voice A plays one 8-note mallet pattern from the title on. Voice B enters at the list and slips a 16th every 1.5 bars (phasing).
