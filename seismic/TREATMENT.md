# Treatment: "What should remain private?"

**Brief:** a 16:9 cinematic motion film for the Seismic post (TEEs + shielded Solidity types vs ZK chains), in the brand colours (Mauve `#825A6D`, Purple `#523542`, greys `#FFFFFF`–`#161616`) with the logo. Not a slideshow.

## Structure
Three candidates: (1) a straight explainer list ZK → TEE → goal; (2) a fork in the road, two paths, one question; (3) a countdown of privacy facts.
**Chosen: (2), the fork.** The post itself is a comparison that ends on a question, so the film is a road that splits and comes back to one point of light.

**Logline:** a chain of blocks splits into two paths to privacy (proofs and enclaves); Seismic lands like an earthquake on the second one, and both paths come back to a single question.

- **Setup:** a seismograph line wakes up and the stone (the logo) assembles from shards.
- **Turn:** the logo slams into a grid and a seismic wave rolls out ("a different path").
- **Ending:** both approaches converge into one point of light, which bursts into the question; the stone and the seismograph return (a bookend).

**Native move (the peak):** the seismic wave. The brand name is a real thing on screen: the logo hits a field of blocks and the shockwave physically lifts them in mauve light.

## Benchmark (grammar only)
Premium launch films and dark keynote reveals: a black stage, one accent, light as the reveal tool, and type that rises out of focus. What I didn't take: any silhouettes, UI, fonts, logos or melodies from those films.

## Cue map (96 BPM, bar = 2.5 s, everything on bar lines)
- Hits (shake + chromatic split, three in the whole film): **2.5, 25, 55**. White flash at **37.5**.
- Silences: **24.55–25** and **54.3–55**. The first sound after each silence is the hit.
- Score: D minor (i–VI–III–VII); an F major lift for "The goal?"; the pulse divides from 8ths to 32nds before the final question.
